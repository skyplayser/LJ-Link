Features

Built for the lightjockey

See at a glance what the DJ loads, where each track is and when the next drop comes – even when two DJs play on different gear.

▤All decks in one view

Stacked in a familiar player layout: cover, title, time to the millisecond, remaining time, pitch, BPM, key, master, sync and the model of each player.

◧Serato DJ Pro via LJL Link (alpha)

The DJ starts LJL Link from the club’s USB stick on the Mac with Serato DJ Pro. LJ Link in the booth gets track, length, BPM, key, waveforms and position, next to every other player. New in 1.7.11, not tested in a club yet.

More about LJL Link →

≋Waveforms like on the player

Scrolling detail waveform and whole-track overview with hot cues A–H, memory cues, orange loops and the beat grid with red bar starts. RGB, 3-band or blue.

●LIVE frame

The deck that is on air gets a red LIVE frame, so you never light the wrong track.

⇄Every maker, several devices

CDJs, XDJs and Denon controllers appear side by side. A device that joins later is added by itself, one that leaves disappears. Tick devices on or off.

♫Denon with waveforms

Prime 4, Prime 4+, Prime Go and SC6000: title, tempo, exact position, real fader positions and the waveform with beat grid and cues from the Engine library.

♩BPM to the light desk

Sends the tempo to grandMA2 and grandMA2 onPC (Telnet), grandMA3 and onPC (OSC), to Daslight (Ableton Link or OSC tap), to the Wolfmix (Ableton Link or OS2L, beta), and as an alpha to ChamSys MagicQ (OSC taps). The SEND BPM tab picks Auto or one deck.

◈VJ-Link connection

LJ Link feeds tempo, beat, phrases, hot cues, title, cover and waveform to VJ-Link, so visuals run in time, change scene on the drop and show what is playing. Finds itself in the network.

⎍Mixer tab

One block per mixer with ON AIR per channel; on Denon with the real fader positions. Pro DJ Link does not transmit faders or EQs.

▢Tablet, phone, second screen

Open the same page on any device in the network. Day and night mode, zoom, three views, full screen.

◐Privacy mode

For streams and shared screens: title, artist and cover disappear, on tablets too. Switched only from the program icon.

✉︎Suite chat (alpha)

Light desk, DJ booth and VJ write to each other in the same network, without a server, with the other suite programs such as VJ-Link and LJL Link. EMERGENCY shows a pop-up at everyone at once. Only on the computer running LJ Link, can be switched off, never leaves the local network. From 1.7.11, not yet tested between two computers or on Windows.

↻One-click updates

Check for updates from the program icon. On a Mac the app is replaced and restarts, on Windows the installer opens.

✓Simple for everyone

One button finds everything. If it finds nothing, the manual setup checks the network step by step. Demo mode without hardware.

⊘Passive and private

Only listens: no sync, master, tempo or fader commands to the DJ gear. One exception, off by default: the optional Ableton Link output leads the tempo of a Link session, and Link programs in it follow – including DJ software such as Traktor if it joins the session. No telemetry, no cloud.

New for Serato DJ ProAlpha

Serato DJ Pro? With LJL Link in the booth

Serato DJ Pro has no public interface that LJ Link could use for decks, waveforms and position. LJL Link – LJ LAN Link – is a separate small Mac app for users of Serato DJ Pro: started from the club’s USB stick, it sends the DJ’s decks to LJ Link in the booth, next to every other player. Free and open source.

◉Who it’s for

DJs with Serato DJ Pro on a Mac, and the lighting team that wants to light to their music. The LJ sees the Serato decks in LJ Link like the other players, and LJ Link does not have to run on the DJ’s computer.

⇢How it works

Runs from the club’s USB stick, nothing to install. LJL Link passively reads Serato’s log file, the loaded track and the time display in Serato’s window, and sends deck, length, BPM, key, waveforms and position. It never controls Serato and never touches controllers or MIDI. Track names only go out with the DJ’s OK.

✉︎Booth chat

LJL Link, LJ Link and VJ-Link in the same network share one chat room, without a server: type instead of shouting across the dance floor. EMERGENCY shows a pop-up at everyone. Not encrypted, every device in the network can read along; EMERGENCY is not an alarm or safety system.

Alpha: tested locally on one Mac with Serato DJ Pro 5.0, not yet in a club. Needs macOS 14 or newer on the DJ’s Mac and LJ Link 1.7.11 or newer in the booth. Not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS warns on the first start. Keep a fallback ready, for example tap tempo on the desk.

Light desk

BPM from your CDJs to grandMA – Denon too

LJ Link sends the BPM of the leading deck to your desk, so chasers and effects run in time. No tap tempo needed.

  • grandMA2 / onPC – over Telnet, with a search for the desk. Speed 1–15 or Speed 16 = BPM.
  • grandMA3 / onPC – over OSC as a command line.
  • Daslight – over Ableton Link (recommended, with beat and bar) or as an OSC tap on BPM Tap. Works with Daslight.
  • Other OSC programs – the tempo as an OSC number.
  • ChamSys MagicQ – an OSC tap on every beat for Tap to Time (alpha, untested).
  • Wolfmix – over Ableton Link (recommended) or every beat over OS2L and WTOOLS (beta).
  • Ableton Link – leads the Link session with the deck’s tempo, beats and bars, for Resolume, Ableton Live and Link apps. Works with Daslight.
  • SEND BPM – Auto (master and/or live) or one deck by hand; the sending deck shows a green SEND BPM mark.

Install

First start: getting past the warning

LJ Link is not signed with Apple or Microsoft (yet), so both systems warn once on the first start. Signing costs money; without it, macOS or Windows cannot verify the developer. So only download the program from the official page. Here is how to continue – after that the program starts normally.

⌘macOS

Message: “LJ Link” Not Opened – Apple could not verify “LJ Link” is free of malware.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag “Link-Suite” into the Applications folder.
  2. Start Link-Suite once. The warning appears: click “Done” (not “Move to Trash”).
  3. Open System Settings → “Privacy & Security” → scroll all the way down.
  4. Next to “Link-Suite was blocked …” click “Open Anyway” and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  5. In the next window choose “Open” once more.
  6. In the launcher click “Install” next to LJ Link. Updates later come through the launcher as well, without the warning.
  7. When macOS asks about the local network: “Allow”. Without it LJ Link cannot find any players.

On older macOS versions (up to 14) this also works: right-click the app → “Open” → “Open”.

For experts: with Terminal

After copying to Applications, run this command in Terminal, then the app starts without the warning:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Link-Suite.app"

⊞Windows

Message: “Windows protected your PC” (Microsoft Defender SmartScreen).

  1. Run Link-Suite-windows.exe.
  2. In the blue window click “More info”.
  3. “Run anyway” appears at the bottom: click it.
  4. Click through the installer and start Link-Suite. In the launcher click “Install” next to LJ Link – LJ Link appears in the Start menu and on the desktop.
  5. On the first start the Windows Firewall asks: tick “Private networks” and choose “Allow access”. Without it LJ Link cannot find any players.

Missed the firewall question? Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → “Allow an app through firewall” → tick LJ Link for “Private”.

Some virus scanners flag unknown, unsigned programs as a precaution. The installer is only offered here and on GitHub; when in doubt, download it there.

After the first start the LJ Link icon appears in the menu bar (Mac) or next to the clock (Windows). Use it to open the interface, check for updates and quit.

FAQ

Questions

What is a lighting jockey (LJ)?

A lighting jockey runs the light show live to the DJ's music – in clubs, at festivals and at events. To match light and music, the LJ needs to know what the DJ is playing and when the next drop comes. That is exactly what LJ Link shows.

Does LJ Link work with Serato?

Yes, with Serato DJ Pro in two ways. On the same computer, LJ Link reads the loaded tracks, BPM, key, waveform overview, beat grid and cues, but without play position: Serato DJ Pro has no public interface that LJ Link could use for it. Over the network, the separate Mac app LJL Link (alpha) sends track, length, BPM, key, waveforms and position from the DJ’s Mac to LJ Link 1.7.11 or newer in the booth; tested locally on one Mac with Serato DJ Pro 5.0, not yet in a club. LJ Link and LJL Link are independent projects, not affiliated with Serato. More: Serato DJ Pro in the wiki · LJL Link in the wiki.

Does it work with older CDJs and mixers too?

Yes. LJ Link runs with the CDJ-2000NXS2 and the DJM-2000NXS. Other Pro DJ Link gear like the CDJ-2000NXS, CDJ-900NXS, XDJ-1000 or DJM-900NXS is expected to work but not tested yet.

How do I get the BPM from the CDJs into my grandMA?

LJ Link reads the tempo over Pro DJ Link (Pioneer) or StageLinQ (Denon) and sends it over Telnet to grandMA2 or over OSC to grandMA3, onto a speed master. Chasers and effects then run in time, without tap tempo.

Can I see the DJ's waveform on a tablet at the light desk?

Yes. Any device in the same network – tablet, phone or second screen – opens the same view in the browser, with day and night mode.

Does LJ Link need internet?

No. For the show it only needs the local network between DJ gear, computer and light desk. No cloud, no telemetry.

Does LJ Link disturb the DJ or the players?

No. It only listens and sends no sync, master, tempo or fader commands to the DJ gear. On Pro DJ Link it joins as a virtual player, like other programs do. One exception, off by default: if you switch on the Ableton Link output, DJ software that joins the same Link session (such as Traktor) follows LJ Link’s tempo.

Why do I not see fader positions on a Pioneer mixer?

Pro DJ Link only transmits whether a channel is on air. No DJM sends faders or EQ. Denon devices do report fader positions.

Why does a track have no waveform?

Pioneer: tracks must be analysed with rekordbox and exported to USB/SD; streaming tracks have no waveform data. Denon: the waveform comes from the device's Engine library.

Can I show two DJs with different gear?

Yes. One press of “Search automatically” finds Pioneer and Denon at the same time, and devices that come or go later are added or removed by themselves.

Does it work with my dot2?

Not yet: the dot2 takes neither Telnet nor OSC over the network. A way via MIDI is planned.

My Mac says “LJ Link” Not Opened – what does that mean?

The app is not signed with Apple (yet), so macOS cannot verify the developer. Only download LJ Link from the official GitHub page (github.com/skyplayser/LJ-Link) or via this website. How to open it then is explained above under “Install”.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free and open source under the Eclipse Public License 2.0.
