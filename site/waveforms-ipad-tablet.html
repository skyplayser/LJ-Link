See the drops coming from wherever you stand: the waveforms of every Denon, Pioneer and Serato deck in the browser of your iPad. No app, free.

Short answer

LJ Link runs on the computer in the DJ network and shows its picture as a web page. Join the same Wi-Fi with an iPad, Android tablet or phone, open the address LJ Link shows, and you see the waveforms, decks, mixer and BPM live, with Denon Prime (StageLinQ), Pioneer CDJs and Serato side by side.

Set up in four steps

  1. Computer with LJ Link by cable in the DJ network.
  2. Wi-Fi for the tablet: a router or access point on the same switch, not the guest network.
  3. Under the toolbar LJ Link shows its address. Open it in the tablet's browser.
  4. Full screen, night mode and add to the home screen on the tablet.

If the computer only has 169.254.x.x addresses (no router), LJ Link shows no address: add a router or access point.

Denon, Pioneer and Serato on one screen

  • Denon Prime and SC6000: waveforms from the Engine library on the device, over StageLinQ.
  • Pioneer CDJs and XDJs: the waveforms rekordbox wrote onto the USB stick, shared over Pro DJ Link.
  • Serato DJ Pro through LJL Link: the overview and the beat grid, without a playhead.

Questions

Do I need an app on the iPad?

No. LJ Link shows its address, for example http://192.168.1.50:8080, and you open it in the tablet's browser. Add it to the home screen for one tap next time.

Which DJ gear can I see on the tablet?

Pioneer CDJs and XDJs, Denon Prime players (StageLinQ) and Serato DJ Pro decks through LJL Link, next to each other.

Can the tablet control LJ Link?

It shows everything and has its own view settings (zoom, colours, day or night, language). Starting or stopping LJ Link and changing SEND BPM stays on the computer.

Why does the iPad screen lock?

Without HTTPS, Safari has no reliable screen lock on an iPad. Connect it to power and set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock to Never for the gig.

Free for macOS and Windows

LJ Link is free and open source. Install it, press “Search automatically” and you are live.
