Light desks · alphaDaslight

A tap on every beat for BPM TAP. Untested.

!Status

Untested alpha feature: never tried on a real Daslight. LJ Link sends the taps as described; whether your Daslight version takes them on the BPM TAP button as expected has to be checked. Please report how it went.

How it works

Daslight cannot take the tempo as an OSC number and apply it as BPM. But its OSC mapping can trigger buttons, and Daslight has a BPM TAP button. So LJ Link sends an OSC message without a value on every beat of the leading deck, and you map that address to BPM TAP. Daslight then measures the tempo from the beats, just as if you tapped it.

Set it up

  1. In Daslight switch on the OSC input and note the port.

  2. In LJ Link, in the light desk settings: Daslight (OSC taps), address of the Daslight computer (same computer: 127.0.0.1), port, keep the address /ljlink/tap. Save.

  3. Press Send test. In Daslight check the OSC monitor for /ljlink/tap.

  4. In the OSC mapping assign /ljlink/tap to the BPM TAP button.

  5. Play a deck. The BPM in Daslight should follow after a few beats.

Good to know

  • The tempo is only as exact as Daslight's tap averaging. It needs a few beats after every tempo change.
  • The menu names depend on your Daslight version. If the mapping offers no BPM TAP target, tell us which version you have.
  • Alternatives: Ableton Link in Daslight with a Link-capable DJ software (Serato, Traktor), or with Pioneer and Denon players through LJ Link, which can now lead the Link session (alpha, untested).
  • A tempo sent as a plain OSC number does not work with Daslight; use the generic OSC target only for programs that read such a number.

▶Help us test

Tried it on your desk? Post the result in our Discord or report it: desk, software version, what worked and what did not. That is how beta and alpha become stable.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
