Light desks · unconfirmedDaslight

The tempo as an OSC number.

!Status

Unconfirmed: LJ Link sends, but whether your Daslight version can put an OSC number on its BPM is not known yet.

How it works

Daslight syncs its tempo officially via tap, MIDI clock and Ableton Link, and has an OSC mapping for incoming messages. LJ Link sends the tempo as a number over OSC, for example 128.0 to /ljlinkpro/bpm.

Try it

  1. In Daslight switch on the OSC input and note the port.

  2. In LJ Link: Daslight, address of the Daslight computer (same computer: 127.0.0.1), port, keep the address /ljlinkpro/bpm. Save, Send test.

  3. In Daslight check the MIDI/OSC monitor for /ljlinkpro/bpm.

  4. In the OSC mapping assign the address to the BPM or speed control, if your version offers it.

If it cannot be assigned

  • Use Ableton Link in Daslight: with a Link-capable DJ software (Serato, Traktor), or with Pioneer and Denon players through LJ Link, which can now lead the Link session (alpha).
  • Tap tempo, with the big BPM field in LJ Link as a guide.
  • A MIDI clock straight from LJ Link is planned.

▶Help us test

Tried it on your desk? Post the result in our Discord or report it: desk, software version, what worked and what did not. That is how beta and alpha become stable.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
