Daslight, any program that takes OSC, and the dot2.

Daslight

Unconfirmed

Daslight officially syncs its tempo by tap tempo, MIDI clock and Ableton Link. It also has an OSC mapping that assigns incoming OSC messages to controls. LJ Link sends the tempo as a number over OSC (for example 128.0 to /ljlinkpro/bpm). Whether this can set the BPM in your Daslight version is not confirmed yet. How to try:

  1. In Daslight switch the OSC input on and note the port (in the settings or in the OSC mapping).
  2. In LJ Link choose Daslight, enter the IP address of the Daslight computer (same computer: 127.0.0.1) and the port, keep the address /ljlinkpro/bpm, press Save and Send test.
  3. In the MIDI/OSC monitor of Daslight check whether /ljlinkpro/bpm arrives.
  4. In the OSC mapping assign the address to the BPM or speed control, if your version offers that.

If the message arrives but no BPM can be assigned, only tap tempo or MIDI clock from another source is left for now. A MIDI clock straight from LJ Link is planned.

Custom OSC target (Resolume, QLC+, MagicQ …)

Generic

Custom OSC target sends the tempo as a number (float) to an address and port you choose, for example for Resolume, QLC+, Chamsys MagicQ, TouchDesigner or your own scripts. In the target program assign this address to a tempo control.

Whether it works depends on the receiving program. For programs that understand OS2L, such as QLC+ with its OS2L plugin, see also the Wolfmix page: the same OS2L output can be pointed at them (alpha).

dot2

Not possible

The dot2 cannot be controlled over the network, neither by Telnet nor by OSC. According to its manual it takes only MIDI, DMX (also Art-Net and sACN) and switch contacts from outside, so LJ Link cannot send it the tempo today. What you can do instead:

  • Tap tempo: hold the Speed key and tap in time on the key of an executor. The big BPM display in LJ Link helps to count along.
  • Sound to light: the dot2 has an audio input with BPM detection (window Sound Input).

A way over MIDI is planned for a later version.

Tried it? Tell us

Every report helps to turn a beta or alpha into a tested feature, also when it does not work. In LJ Link open the menu (☰) → “Report a problem” and send it by email or on GitHub. Please add your device, its firmware, the software version and what you saw.
