How it works in short

  1. LJ Link listens passively to your DJ gear on the network (or, for Serato, to the same computer). It never controls the gear.
  2. It shows live waveforms, tracks and decks in the browser, also on a tablet.
  3. Optionally it sends the tempo of the leading deck to a light desk, so chasers and effects run in time with the music.

What the status means

Every device and program in this wiki carries one of these labels.

StatusMeaning
StableTested on the real device.
BetaBuilt from the maker’s manual and tested against a simulation, but not yet confirmed on a real device. Please report how it went.
AlphaNew and never tried on the real device. It may not work at all. Do not rely on it at an important event.
UnconfirmedLJ Link sends, but it is not known whether the program can use it.
Not possibleThe device cannot receive this from a network.
