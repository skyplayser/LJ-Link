StartThe interface

What every element on the screen means.

Waveform view

Every deck is a row: on the left the data of the player, on the right the waveforms.

ElementMeaning
PLAYER / TRACKplayer number and track number on the medium
Big timeelapsed time in minutes, seconds and milliseconds, below REMAIN
TEMPOpitch in percent
BPM boxcurrent tempo, orange on the tempo master
MT / SYNC / PAUSEmaster, sync on, paused
Red LIVE framethe deck is on air at the mixer
Blue bar top leftmixer channel: on air or off with Pioneer, the real fader level with Denon
Upper waveformscrolls past the playhead, the played part is darker
Lower waveformthe whole track with playhead and remaining time
Flags A–H, red triangleshot cues in their colours, memory cues
Orange area ↻ 8loop, the active one brighter, with its length in beats
Ticks, red onesbeat grid, red at the start of a bar
Green SEND BPMthis deck gives the tempo to the light desk

A click on a deck opens everything the player reports: device, status, tempo, loop, medium, track, position, cues and the raw packet.

Toolbar and views

  • Zoom − / +: how many seconds the upper waveform shows.
  • VIEW: both waveforms, detail only or overview only.
  • Tabs WAVEFORM and MIXER; LINK opens the light desk link, SEND BPM chooses the deck for the tempo.
  • Full screen for the desk.

Mixer tab

✎Note

Pro DJ Link only tells which channel is on air. No DJM sends fader positions or EQ. With a Pioneer mixer the bar shows on air or off and the knobs stay empty. Denon devices send the real fader levels, but no EQ. The demo simulates both and marks it SIMULATED.

Settings

  • Waveform colours: RGB like on the player or classic blue.
  • Display: day or night.
  • Language: English or German, saved per browser.
  • Devices: with many devices or several floors tick devices on or off.
  • Light desk link: see light desks.
  • Ableton Link: lead the Link session with the DJ tempo, beats and bars (works with Daslight), see Ableton Link.
  • Screen → Keep screen awake – Alpha: the screen of this device stays on while LJ Link is shown. On by default, saved per browser. See below.
  • Updates → Check for updates automatically: shown only on the computer running LJ Link, see updates.
  • Quit → Show note when quitting – Alpha (new in 1.7.9): shown only on the computer running LJ Link and only while the program icon is shown, see quitting.
  • Feedback → Report a problem or request a feature: opens the report window, see troubleshooting.
  • Disconnect ends the running connection.

Keep the screen awake

!Warning

New in 1.7.6 and alpha: works on a Mac. On the iPad it has not kept the screen on reliably so far; Android tablets and Windows PCs are not tested yet. For iPad and iPhone see the reliable way.

In a booth nobody touches the device for hours, so screens dim and lock. Menu (☰) → Screen → Keep screen awake – Alpha prevents that. It is on by default and stays as set in each browser, also after a reload. The line below the setting shows the status.

WhereWhat happens
Browser on the computer with LJ Link (http://localhost:8080)the screen stays on at once, no tap needed. Status: Active – the screen stays on
iPad or tablet over the network (http://<address>:8080)a strip right below the header shows ☼ Tap here once to keep the screen on. Tap the strip or anywhere else on the page once, then LJ Link tries to keep the screen on: the status shows Starting the video …, after a few seconds Active (video) – the screen stays on. If the video does not start (after about 6 seconds) or iOS stops it, the status shows Video stopped – please tap the page and the strip says ☼ The screen may turn off – tap here to restart keep-awake; tap once more. Without HTTPS this is only a workaround (alpha) that has not been reliable on the iPad so far; there use auto-lock Never, see the reliable way
Page hidden (other tab, app or minimised)Status: Paused while the page is hidden. When you come back, the last state applies again; if the video was stopped meanwhile, the status shows Video stopped – please tap the page, then tap once more
Setting offthe device dims and locks as usual. Status: Off

All status texts below the setting (shown as „Status: …“):

StatusMeaning
Active – the screen stays onthe browser's own screen lock is on (computer itself or HTTPS)
Waiting for a tap – tap the page onceover the network; nothing runs until the first tap
Starting the video …after the tap, LJ Link checks whether the silent video really plays
Active (video) – the screen stays onthe video plays; LJ Link tries to keep the screen on this way (alpha, on the iPad not reliable so far)
Video stopped – please tap the pagethe video did not start within about 6 seconds or was stopped; tap once more
Paused while the page is hiddenother tab or app; continues when you come back
Not available in this browserneither way works here; set auto-lock on the device to Never
Offsetting switched off

On the video way a note below the setting adds: on an iPad or iPhone without HTTPS this is a stopgap; more reliable is auto-lock Never with the charger connected, and Low Power Mode off (on the iPhone it shortens auto-lock to 30 seconds, probably on the iPad too). See the reliable way.

On a Mac, LJ Link itself also keeps the computer from falling asleep while at least one browser is connected, also an iPad, so the tablet does not lose LJ Link. About a minute after the last browser is closed the Mac may sleep again. Whether the Mac display stays on is up to the page in its browser. On Windows LJ Link does not do this part yet.

Screen goes dark anyway? See troubleshooting.

Privacy mode

Switched only from the program icon. LJ Link then sends no titles, artists, albums, genres, labels, comments or covers at all, also not to tablets. Waveforms, times, tempo and cues stay. Details: livestream and privacy mode.

Demo mode

Watch demo on the start screen shows three simulated players with moving waveforms, mixer values and cues. Good for trying the interface at home. The demo never sends anything to a light desk.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
